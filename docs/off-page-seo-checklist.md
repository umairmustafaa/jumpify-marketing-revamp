# Off-Page & Backlink SEO Checklist — Faisal Town Phase 2

**Goal:** Rank #1 for "Faisal Town Phase 2" and related terms; drive qualified leads.

**Context (from competitor research):** On-page is now best-in-class (crawlable price tables,
honest NOC section, schema, dedicated block pages, blog cluster). The remaining bottleneck is
**off-page authority (backlinks) + freshness**. Exact-match-domain microsites and dealer peers
(Wirasat, Makaan, Manahil, Win Win) mostly beat newcomers on *age and links*, not content.
This checklist closes that gap. Work top-to-bottom; the first section is non-negotiable.

> Update `site.url` to the live domain before any of this. Track progress by ticking `[x]`.

## 2. Backlinks — the real ranking lever

Aim for a steady trickle of *relevant, real* links — quality over quantity. ~2–4 new links/month
beats a burst. Never buy spammy link packages (Google penalises them).

### Easy / foundational
- [ ] **YouTube video descriptions** — publish FT2 site-visit / payment-plan / block-tour videos and
      put your page URL (deep-linked to the relevant block) in every description. Video also ranks in Google.
- [ ] **Social posts** linking to specific pages (blocks, blog articles) — not just the homepage.
- [ ] **WhatsApp Business** + broadcast/status linking to blog articles (drives traffic + shares).
- [ ] **Quora / Reddit** — genuinely answer "Is Faisal Town Phase 2 approved?" style questions and
      cite your honest NOC article as a source (do not spam).

### Medium effort
- [ ] **Guest posts** on Pakistani property/real-estate blogs — offer a genuinely useful article
      (e.g. "Investing near the New Islamabad Airport") with one contextual link back.
- [ ] **Local news / press** — a short press release on a project milestone or your agency, syndicated
      to PK PR sites, earns links.
- [ ] **Business associations / chambers** — Rawalpindi/Islamabad real estate associations, member directories.
- [ ] **Partner/vendor links** — developers, builders, mortgage/legal partners linking to you.

### Higher effort / high value
- [ ] **Comparison & data content** others cite — your "FT2 vs Faisal Hills" and "NOC status" articles
      are link-worthy; actively pitch them to bloggers/YouTubers covering the project.
- [ ] **Digital PR** — offer honest commentary (esp. the NOC/legal angle) to journalists covering
      Islamabad real estate. Honesty on the NOC issue is a differentiator worth pitching.

### Avoid
- [ ] ❌ Paid link farms, PBNs, comment-spam, irrelevant foreign directories, exact-match anchor
      over-optimisation. These risk penalties and waste money.

## 3. Content Freshness Cadence (beats the stale field)

The whole competitor field is stale (some footers still say © 2022; promo deadlines long expired).
A visible freshness habit is a near-free ranking edge.

- [ ] **Monthly:** update the homepage `lastUpdated` date in `lib/ft2.ts` and refresh any changed
      prices/plans. Publish or update one "Development Update — [Month] 2026" blog post.
- [ ] **On every rate revision:** update `residentialPlan` and the block payment notes the same week,
      and add a short "prices revised" blog note. (Dated, refreshed pages rank and earn repeat visits.)
- [ ] **Quarterly:** add 1–2 new cluster articles from the keyword list (below) and re-share old ones.

### Keyword backlog for new articles (topical authority)
`faisal town phase 2 development update` · `faisal town phase 2 vs blue world city` ·
`faisal town phase 2 vs capital smart city` · `faisal town phase 2 balloting` ·
`faisal town phase 2 commercial plots price` · `best block in faisal town phase 2` ·
`faisal town phase 2 possession date` · `faisal town phase 2 owner` (developer track record)
